<h1>Home Helper</h1>
<p>Our website is designed to make life easier for everyone—both the customers who need help and the skilled workers who provide it. Whether someone needs a plumber, a cleaner, an electrician, or support for any other everyday task, the platform quickly searches for the right people nearby and connects them instantly. For customers, it removes the stress of finding reliable help, and for job holders, it creates steady opportunities by notifying them whenever a job matching their skills is available. The goal is simple: to build a trusted space where people can support each other and get things done with ease.</p>
<li>Smart Service Matching: The website automatically connects customers with nearby workers based on the service they need—like plumbing, cleaning, repairs, and more.</li>
<li>User-Friendly Interface: Simple, clean, and easy for both customers and workers to navigate.</li>
<li>No Login System: No requirement for informations.</li>
<li>Profile & Skill Management: Job holders can showcase their skills, experience, and availability to attract more customers.</li>
<li>Job Tracking Dashboard: Customers can track the status of their service request, and workers can manage their tasks.</li>
<li>Flexible Service Categories: Supports a wide range of services—plumbing, cleaning, electrical.</li>
